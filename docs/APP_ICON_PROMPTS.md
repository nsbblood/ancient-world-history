## Design Guidelines for All Icons:

### Color Palette:
- **Primary Gold**: #D4AF37 (luxurious, historical)
- **Bronze/Dark Gold**: #8B6914 (depth, antiquity)
- **Background Dark**: #1A1C21 (modern, premium)
- **Background Darker**: #0D0E12 (depth)
- **Accent Parchment**: #F7F4EC (warmth, ancient texts)

### Style Requirements:
- Clean, minimalist design that works at 1024x1024 and scales to 20x20
- No text or words
- Premium, sophisticated aesthetic
- Historical theme without being overly detailed
- Recognizable instantly
- Works well in both light and dark mode
- Gradient backgrounds for depth
- Golden accents for luxury feel

### App Identity:
The icon should convey:
- Ancient civilizations and history
- Knowledge and storytelling
- Premium educational content
- Mystery and discovery
- Timeless elegance

## Usage Instructions:
1. Use these prompts with AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion)
2. Generate multiple variations of each
3. Test at different sizes (1024px, 512px, 180px, 60px, 20px)
4. Ensure icon is recognizable at all sizes
5. Choose the one that best represents the app's essence
6. Export in required iOS formats (1024x1024 PNG without transparency)
